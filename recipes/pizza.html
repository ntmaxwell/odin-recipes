Cast Iron Deep Dish Pizza

Home

Description

This is just about my favorite pizza I've ever eaten add it is so easy to make at home. I use Flieschemann's instant pizza dough yeast, but you can use your own recipe for pizza dough if you prefer. Really, this is just about cutting some veggies and assembling the pizza, because I'm a bit lazy. I don't make my own mozzarella cheese or my own pizza sauce, this is meant to be easy, quick and delicious and it is exactly that! A few details I give in the instructions will really elevate the experience!

Ingredients

For the dough

  • Flour
  • Water
  • Yeast
  • Salt
  • Sugar
  • Olive oil

Toppings

Really, this is up to you. What do you like on your pizza? I'm going to show you what I enjoy!

  • Green Bell Peppers
  • Red Onions
  • Portobello Mushrooms
  • Pizza Sauce
  • Mozzarella (I use a block of mozz and either cube it or shred it.)

Really, just use whatever toppings you prefer, but the mozz and the pizza sauce are a must!

Instructions

  1. Make and kneed your dough. Let it rise as needed.
  2. Preheat your oven to 425 farenheit
  3. One option, which I do sometimes and others I dont, is to preheat your cast iron skillet in the oven. This will result in a crispier crust. I usually like to do this. Five minutes is enough.
  4. Add a good amount of olive oil to the bottom of the skillet before adding the dough.
  5. Careful now, because the oil will be hot. Add your dough to the skillet and slowly kneed it out towards the edges, leaving a small crust. Add your mozzarella now and use it to kind of hold the dough in place as you kneed it out.
  6. Add your toppings to the pizza.
  7. Now add your sauce on top. Sauce goes last
  8. Into the oven at 425 degrees for 23-30 minutes.
  9. I normally pull mine out at 25 minutes and it is perfect.
  10. ENJOY!
